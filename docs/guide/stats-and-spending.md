# Stats and spending

Open **Insights** from the library page or the sidebar.

## Period

Choose **All time**, **This year** or **Last 12 months**. Year-based ranges also show a comparison with the previous period.

## Overview

The overview reads the shape of the collection:

- total releases, artists, tracks and genres
- formats, eras and top genres
- **Collection DNA**: top formats, decades and genres
- **By format** and **By decade** breakdowns
- top artists and acquisition stats
- **Needs attention**: records missing a genre, purchase data or metadata, with a link back to the filtered library

Select a format or decade row to open the matching library filter.

## Spending

Switch to **Spending** to see:

- total spent in the selected period
- average and median price
- amount spent this year
- spending over time
- most expensive record
- spending by format
- favourite shops

Spending is grouped by currency. Use the currency selector when the collection contains more than one.

## Improve the numbers

Spending only uses purchase data you entered:

- purchase price
- purchase currency
- purchase date
- purchase location

Add them when creating or editing a release. The library's **Review → Missing price or date** filter finds the gaps.

Formats, decades and genres come from release metadata. Use **Download metadata** or **Search match** to fill missing values.
